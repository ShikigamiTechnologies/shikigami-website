Bounded engagement

One workflow. Ten governed steps. A fixed stop point.

01–02

Intake and boundary

Classify the problem, authority, restricted data, unknowns, urgency and desired outcome before evidence enters the engagement.

03–05

Preserve and reconstruct

Admit approved evidence with provenance, normalize documents and events, and rebuild the workflow without silently resolving conflicts.

06–07

Separate and review

Keep measured facts separate from projections and assumptions. Route high-risk findings to named human review.

08–10

Deliver and decide

Produce executive and technical packages. The customer and Ricardo approve any implementation; nothing consequential occurs automatically.

Decision package

Executive clarity and technical evidence, together.

Every finding retains its source, status and decision boundary.

Executive report

Current-state narrative, material findings, decision options and an owner-reviewed readout.

Technical evidence package

Manifest, hashes, source classification, timestamps, conflicts and correction history.

Improvement plan

Prioritized recommendations, exclusions, acceptance criteria and an explicit stop point.

Measured

Observed values supported by admitted evidence and reproducible calculations.

Projected

Future effects modeled separately, with method and range visible.

Assumed

Inputs supplied for planning but not independently established.

Unknown

Missing evidence that remains unresolved instead of being estimated away.

Authority and data boundary

Evidence in. Recommendations out. Human authority remains.
