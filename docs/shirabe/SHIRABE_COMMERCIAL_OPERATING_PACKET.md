# SHIRABE Commercial Operating Packet

Status: internal controlled candidate. Human legal, privacy, tax, insurance, and accounting review required before use. This packet does not authorize outreach, customer data, contracting, payment, deployment, or publication.

## Sellable unit

One bounded bilingual workflow diagnostic that produces:

- an evidence-linked current-state workflow map;
- an evidence, conflict, assumption, and unknowns register;
- a deterministic low/base/high opportunity ledger;
- an implementation-neutral recommendation to change, buy, build, collect more evidence, or do nothing; and
- a portable, hash-verifiable English/Spanish decision package.

SHIRABE is not an audit, certification, legal opinion, fraud determination, autonomous operator, process-mining platform, or guarantee of savings.

## Fixed engagement boundary

- One named workflow and one decision question.
- Named executive sponsor, workflow owner, evidence custodian, and SHIRABE engagement director.
- No credentials, connectors, background monitoring, production writes, or automated operational action.
- Evidence is admitted only through the engagement authority and data-boundary records.
- New workflows, systems, jurisdictions, sensitive-data classes, or desired outcomes require a written change decision.
- Work stops on missing authority, prohibited data, unresolved safety risk, exhausted time/cost boundary, or owner withdrawal.

## Draft contractual clauses requiring counsel review

### Evidence and authority

The customer represents that it is authorized to supply each item and identify any handling restriction. Receipt is not admission. SHIRABE may reject, quarantine, minimize, or request replacement evidence. Customer assertions and interviews remain self-reports until independently supported.

### Decision responsibility

SHIRABE supplies evidence-bounded options. The customer retains all operational, employment, financial, legal, procurement, security, and implementation decisions. No AI worker may approve evidence, findings, customer actions, or final delivery.

### No audit or outcome warranty

The engagement is a bounded operational diagnostic and not an audit, attestation, certification, legal opinion, forensic investigation, or guarantee of savings, compliance, performance, or implementation success.

### Corrections and disputes

Corrections create a new version. Prior versions remain identifiable in the audit record. Disputed claims remain visible as conflicts unless a named authorized human records a disposition.

### Confidentiality, privacy, and subprocessors

The signed agreement must identify permitted data classes, purpose, location, transfer, access roles, approved subprocessors/providers, incident notice, return/deletion, backup aging, and applicable law. No provider receives engagement evidence unless named and approved.

### Intellectual property

The customer retains its source evidence. The agreement must distinguish customer-specific deliverables from SHIRABE's pre-existing method, schemas, validation utilities, and generic know-how. Cross-customer evidence or training is prohibited by default.

## Acceptance gate

Delivery may be presented for acceptance only when:

- every material finding links to admitted evidence;
- calculations reproduce from versioned inputs;
- conflicts and unknowns remain visible;
- material EN/ES findings, exclusions, risks, and numbers are semantically equivalent;
- independent verification is recorded;
- package and manifest hashes verify;
- retention/deletion instructions are recorded; and
- named humans approve scope closure and final delivery.

- legal/accounting/insurance allocation;
- acquisition and support burden;

- variables capable of making the engagement uneconomic.

## Truthful claim ledger

Allowed now:

- Controlled bilingual diagnostic intake is available.
- The method has been exercised with synthetic internal scenarios.
- Findings distinguish evidence, self-report, inference, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Customer decisions remain human-controlled.

Held until design-partner evidence:

- Repeatable paid-service readiness.
- Customer savings, ROI, accuracy, time reduction, or satisfaction.
- Market leadership or superiority.
- Production, audit, compliance, certification, or federal-readiness claims.
