# SHIRABE Comparative Qualification Protocol

Status: frozen internal protocol. Synthetic data only. It is designed to discover where SHIRABE is better, worse, or merely different; it cannot establish market demand or customer outcomes.

## Configurations

Run the same three synthetic workflow cases through:

1. SHIRABE's governed diagnostic method.
2. A manual analyst using PM4Py where the supplied evidence permits event-log analysis.
3. One owner-approved free or trial workflow/process platform, with no customer data, credentials, purchase, or publication.

If a platform cannot accept the evidence form, record `not_applicable` rather than converting absence into a SHIRABE win.

## Frozen cases

- Case A: structured event log with clean identifiers and timestamps.
- Case B: fragmented email/PDF/spreadsheet/interview evidence with conflicting timestamps.
- Case C: bilingual fragmented evidence with missing ownership, restricted-data rejection, correction, and an unresolved conflict.

## Measures

- Operator minutes and calendar duration.
- Provider, platform, and infrastructure cost.
- Evidence requested, admitted, rejected, quarantined, and unavailable.
- Material findings with complete provenance.
- Unsupported claims and unsupported causal claims.
- Conflicts found, preserved, human-dispositioned, and silently lost.
- Source coverage and unresolved unknowns.
- Critical English/Spanish semantic divergence.
- Deterministic calculation reproducibility.
- Recommendation options produced and evidence limits stated.
- Export portability, hash verification, clean restore, and deletion.
- Operator correction and rework.

## Stop and failure conditions

Stop the run on customer or non-synthetic data, credential request, paid activation, unauthorized upload, cross-case leakage, evidence mutation, silent conflict resolution, unsupported guilt/fraud/causation language, or an untraceable material finding.

## Interpretation rules

- Faster is not better if provenance or authority fails.
- More findings are not better if claims are unsupported.
- A clean event-log platform is expected to outperform SHIRABE on continuous mining, visualization, simulation, and scale.
- SHIRABE's hypothesis is supported only if it adds measurable value on fragmented-evidence cases without unacceptable labor, cost, or quality loss.
- Tool inability is not customer willingness to pay.
- Synthetic advantage is not a public superiority claim.

## Advancement threshold

Advance to design-partner consideration only if SHIRABE records:

- zero authority and scope violations;
- 100% provenance for material findings;
- zero silently lost conflicts;
- zero unsupported material claims;
- zero severe bilingual divergence;
- successful export, verify, restore, and deletion;
- reproducible deterministic calculations; and
- documented labor and cost consistent with a plausible positive-margin offer.

The final comparative report must publish losses as clearly as wins and label every conclusion as observation, inference, hypothesis, or unknown.
