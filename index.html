01 — Who we are

Human authority is a product requirement, not a disclaimer

Shikigami Technologies is an applied-software company founded and solely owned by Ricardo Parra Pastor. We build for organizations expected to manage the document, contract, compliance, and operational complexity of much larger institutions — without equivalent staff or systems.

Source evidence

Every output points back to the document, clause, or record it came from. No unexplained answers.

Relationships

Records are connected — a promise to its source, its owner, its deadline, and its proof.

Visible uncertainty

Missing facts, assumptions, and exceptions are shown as such — never smoothed into false confidence.

Bounded assistance

Assistance is scoped and permissioned. Consequential action waits for an authorized person and leaves an auditable outcome.

Each card states what the system may do, what a person must decide, and exactly how mature it is today.

Cypher
Document intelligence & operational evidence
Controlled staging / pilot preparation
Explore Cypher →Discuss a pilot →

Invoices, purchase orders, receipts, and correspondence arrive through email, scanners, and shared drives, then fragment. Cypher preserves the original, extracts in English and Spanish, connects related records, surfaces duplicates and exceptions, and supports human correction.

  • What arrived, and what does it say?
  • Which records does it belong with?
  • What is missing, duplicated, or unresolved?
  • Who reviewed or corrected the result?
Intended operator

Multi-location businesses and document-heavy teams responsible for intake, reconciliation, exceptions, and follow-up.

Human authority boundary

Cypher may extract, compare, connect, and flag. People approve corrections, determine business meaning, resolve exceptions, and authorize delivery to external destinations.

Themis
Pre-award contract intelligence
Private beta / active construction
Discuss a pilot →White paper →Request beta →

An opportunity can look attractive while hiding requirements, cash exposure, compliance duties, and delivery risk. Themis maps requirements and clauses to their sources, organizes missing facts and assumptions, and prepares a company-specific recommendation for qualified review.

Can this company finance, execute, comply with, and survive this specific commitment?
Intended operator

Owners, capture teams, contracting personnel, financial reviewers, and subject-matter experts.

Human authority boundary

Themis does not provide legal advice, guarantee outcomes, submit bids, or commit the company. Qualified people decide applicability, risk acceptance, pricing, and pursuit.

ANOR
Post-award assurance & execution control
Early / private development

After an award, contract meaning spreads across project tools, email, drives, modifications, and a few experienced people. ANOR carries that meaning into controlled execution — connecting an obligation to its exact source, approved interpretation, owner, due event, required evidence, and later modifications.

Themis decides before commitment ANOR governs after award

An assurance layer — not a replacement for accounting, payroll, procurement, ERP, legal review, or project management.

Human authority boundary

How Foundry works

The environment

Foundry connects portfolio evidence, repositories, opportunities, workflows, tools, approvals, delivery records, and outcomes in one place, so a decision can be traced to its sources and carried into governed execution. It provides continuity across the company — the memory and wiring, not the judgment.

  • Evidence and repositories in one connected index
  • Workflows, approvals, and delivery records linked
  • Outcomes returned to the decision that caused them
Not a customer-facing product. Not permission for tools to act unattended.
How M.A.G.I. works

The governed second brain

M.A.G.I. plans, delegates bounded work, compares independent analyses, challenges assumptions, synthesizes evidence, verifies results, and prepares decisions for approval. Agreement between models is not authority — it is input to a person who decides.

  • Bounded missions with explicit permissions
  • Independent review, recorded dissent
  • Verification before a recommendation is presented
Not an autonomous executive. It cannot convert a recommendation into company action.
