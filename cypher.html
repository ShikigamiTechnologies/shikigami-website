02 — The operational problem

Documents arrive everywhere. Accountability stays with one organization.

Blurry scans, copied PDFs, inconsistent identifiers, repeated POs, and disconnected spreadsheets turn document control into manual reconstruction.
01

Difficult source files

Image-only and blurry documents defeat ordinary text extraction — and bilingual documents defeat single-language pipelines.

02

Missing relationships

A duplicate invoice is a warning. A reused PO may be legitimate. The difference has to be explained, not guessed.

03

Uncontrolled exceptions

Unmatched documents and confirmed exposure need separate classifications, aging, and an accountable owner.

CONTROL

One defensible record

Source, extraction, matching, validation, evidence, and reporting stay connected — and reproducible later.

03 — How Cypher works

Eight controlled steps.

AI proposes. Deterministic rules compare. Authorized people approve.
01 · UPLOAD

Secure intake

An authorized user submits a document into the customer's private workspace.

02 · PRESERVE

Untouched original

Cypher stores the source privately, hashes it, and opens the audit record.

03 · READ

Layered extraction

Uncertain or incomplete results escalate through additional readers rather than being accepted.

04 · ORGANIZE

Operational context

Store, vendor, document numbers, dates, totals, line items, and configured custom fields become structured data.

05 · MATCH

Rules and relationships

Totals and line items are compared, exact duplicates blocked, and reused POs explained.

06 · REVIEW

The human decision

Original and extracted values appear side by side so the reviewer can confirm, correct, reject, or escalate.

07 · RECORD

Evidence generation

The decision produces a validation PDF, manifest, attribution, timestamp, and immutable audit event.

08 · MONITOR

Operational visibility

Unresolved records, exceptions, aging, stores, and vendors stay visible until someone closes them.
