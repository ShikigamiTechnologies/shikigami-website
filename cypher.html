How Cypher works

From upload to monitored evidence in eight controlled steps.

AI proposes. Deterministic rules compare. Authorized people approve.
01 · UPLOAD

Secure intake

An authorized user submits an invoice or purchase order to the customer’s private workspace.

02 · PRESERVE

Untouched original

Cypher stores the source privately, calculates its SHA-256 hash, and opens the audit record.

03 · READ

Layered extraction

Azure analyzes the scan. Incomplete or uncertain results escalate to Terra and then Sol.

04 · ORGANIZE

Operational context

Tenant, store, vendor, invoice, PO, date, totals, line items, and configured custom fields become structured data.

05 · MATCH

Rules and relationships

Cypher compares totals and line items, blocks exact duplicates, flags duplicate invoices, and explains reused POs.

06 · REVIEW

Secretary decision

The original and extracted values appear together so the authorized reviewer can confirm, correct, reject, or escalate.

07 · RECORD

Evidence generation

The decision creates a validation PDF, evidence manifest, user attribution, timestamp, and immutable audit event.

08 · MONITOR

Operational visibility

Dashboards organize store performance, vendors, unmatched records, confirmed exposure, aging, and exceptions.
