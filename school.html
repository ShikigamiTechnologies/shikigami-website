START SMALL. KEEP YOUR JUDGMENT.

Three volumes. One connected path.

The current reading candidate contains 24 bilingual lessons across three volumes. These describe the curriculum being prepared, not validated learning outcomes.

01

Understand AI

Learn the basics, recognize limits, and check an answer against its sources.

02

Choose a useful problem

Examine a task and decide whether AI, a simple rule, or a person is the right fit.

03

Build a careful workflow

Define steps, review the output, and plan what to do when something fails.

SUPPORT FOR THE LEARNING PROCESS

Room to ask, try, and try again.

A place to begin

A welcome-first path, glossary examples, reflection checks, and guidance for moments when an exercise feels unclear.

Examples before complexity

An OpenAI, ChatGPT, and Codex guide, plus 21 simulated tool workflows. Simulations use fictional data and do not connect accounts or perform real actions.

A path without paid tools

The required exercises are designed to work without coding experience or a paid AI account. Optional tools may have separate costs.
