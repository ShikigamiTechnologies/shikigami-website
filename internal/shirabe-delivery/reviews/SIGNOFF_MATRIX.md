# SHIRABE professional signoff matrix

This is a preparation and evidence-tracking instrument. It does not represent legal, privacy, tax, accounting, insurance, or security approval.

| Discipline | Required reviewer | Current status | Promotion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal | Licensed counsel competent for the engagement jurisdictions | Pending | Named, qualified, dated review of the exact contract and public claims; issues disposition attached |
| Privacy | Qualified privacy counsel or professional | Pending | Exact data flow, retention, deletion, subprocessor and incident terms reviewed |
| Tax/accounting | Puerto Rico-qualified CPA/tax professional plus buyer-jurisdiction advice when needed | Pending | Exact offers, prices, invoice specimen and accounting treatment reviewed |
| Insurance | Licensed broker/carrier representative | Pending | Written coverage/gap mapping for exact services, contracts and data classes |
| Security | Independent qualified security reviewer | Pending | Threat model and negative, recovery, deletion and incident evidence reproduced |
| Bilingual semantics | Native Spanish professional-services reviewer independent of the original copy | Internal technical review completed; independent review pending | Critical EN/ES meaning, numbers, exclusions and authority statements signed off |
| Customer acceptance | Named design-partner sponsor | No engagement exists | Exact final package and correction record accepted in writing |

No worker or automated check may change a professional status to approved. Each signoff requires reviewer identity, qualification, date, exact scope, evidence reference and `self_approved=false`.
