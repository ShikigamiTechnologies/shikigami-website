# SHIRABE bilingual semantic review

Status: internal independent technical-language review; not legal, professional-translation, customer, or market approval.

## Surfaces reviewed

- English and Spanish SHIRABE service pages
- Bilingual public intake structure and language-switching implementation
- Synthetic D2-D4 engagement fixture and critical report statements

## Findings

The English and Spanish service pages preserve the material offer boundary: one controlled diagnostic, evidence provenance, visible measured/projected/assumed/unknown classes, named-human authority, implementation neutrality, three identical price ranges, synthetic-evidence disclosure, and prohibitions against fraud adjudication, automated consequential decisions, credentials, CUI and sensitive public uploads.

The D2-D4 fixture preserves all critical semantic keys and numeric values across languages. Automated checks now reject missing critical statements or numeric divergence. This is necessary but not sufficient: software cannot certify legal nuance, tone or professional translation quality.

### Correction candidates for later copy review

1. `Aprobación humana nombrada` is understandable but unnatural. Prefer `Aprobación por una persona designada` if an independent native reviewer agrees.
2. Spanish uses `costos operacionales`; `costos operativos` may be more natural for a Puerto Rico and broader Latin American business audience. This is a style choice, not a material contradiction.
3. `Admisión de socios de diseño` is literal and may sound clinical. Consider `Solicitud para colaborar como socio de diseño` after marketing and legal review.
4. The public intake translation is JavaScript-mediated within a shared page. Manual keyboard, screen-reader and no-script review is still required in both languages.
5. Currency is displayed as dollars without an explicit `USD` label on the public service price cards. Add or formally confirm the currency before paid promotion.
