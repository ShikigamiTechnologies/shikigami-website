Choose diagnosis when the problem is not yet stable enough to automate.

Choose SHIRABE first

When evidence is fragmented, ownership is unclear and you need a bounded baseline before buying.

Choose a platform

When event data, owners, implementation capacity and a continuing operating model are already defined.

Choose both, in sequence
